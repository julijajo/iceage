# Ice Age of the Karipong
It's been 120 years since our people became entrapped in a Valley. But it wasn't that bad. The snow covered peaks held back something much colder - the IceAge. In your language known as Ice Age, has halted not only the flow of rivers and migration routes of prey animals, but also the connection with the leaders of our people - the Stork Clan. It is unclear whether they perished in the endless white desert or have they found the greener pastures they've promised. We never got a word back, the mountains on all sides stand indifferent to our calls. It is up to us to find new leaders and a new path forward.

## Gameplay
Play as one of the "Enstormed Clans" that are stuck in the valley, exploit uncertainty to make your vision of a future a certainty.

## Features
This is a total conversion mod
- A stunning, lovely, amazing custom map <3
- Completely new culture
  - Including custom traditions, clan dynamics and sprinkled-in lore to be discovered
- Custom religion
  - With new tenets, leadership and power struggle
- Countless characters with their lineages and histories
- and more!
